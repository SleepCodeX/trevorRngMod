# Hand-painted fantasy direction

The user selected **hand-painted fantasy** for advanced module models on 2026-11-02.
A later clarification explicitly preserves the Roll machine and Appearance palette. The other six models should match their clean, crafted style; fantasy surface detail must stay restrained.

Use carved silhouettes, thick readable trim, warm wood against cool enamel, restrained
painted wear, and broad light/shadow shapes. Surface variation is baked; no runtime noise
or texture generation. The default 2D menu artwork remains unchanged.

- Roll: preserve the existing machine geometry, proportions and materials; it is a user-approved visual anchor.
- Crest: the requested deep square prism, with enamel, raised corner hardware and the accessory face image.
- Appearance: preserve the existing organic palette, open thumb hole, paint wells and materials; it is the second visual anchor.
- Layout: layered crafted frames with inset panels and visible depth.
- Jackpots: a thick illustrated gold token with a bold paw and broad rim, without realistic milling.
- Audio: a curved, open gramophone bell and a rounded wood cabinet.
- Pelts: a shaped hide with soft raised leather, edge folds and stitching.
- Keys: a carved compact keyboard with individual sculpted keycaps and a deep case.
